About PORE

Why PORE exists

PORE is not here to promote Pixel Odyssey, or any game. Games quietly take hours that are worth more than any item in them.

So PORE does the slow parts for you: it works out the cheapest way to refine your gear, tells you where every item comes from and what it is used for, and keeps count of what you already have. Less time farming and looking things up; more time for everything else.

If PORE saves you an evening, spend it away from the screen.

PORE stands for Plan Once, Reclaim Evenings. And fittingly, to pore over something is to study it for hours: PORE pores over the wiki, the maps and the numbers, so you don't have to.

How it works

Refining combines two copies of the same item: the base goes up one level and gains stats from the material. Done the standard way, a perfect P10 takes 512 base items.

PORE looks at every other way to combine them. Weaker materials often give the very same stats, so the plan it finds usually needs far fewer base items. Pick an item, choose a level, and PORE walks you through each refine.

PORE plans with the game's refining formula. The game shows every result before you refine, so check it: if it ever shows other numbers, PORE re-plans before you spend anything.

World boss weapons go up to P5 only, and their fees are paid in world boss tokens (WB) instead of gold.

Reading a plan

  • Base items: the plain, unrefined copies to collect. PORE keeps this number as low as it can.
  • Gold: the refining fees for the whole plan.
  • Charms: PORE uses the fewest charms that make each refine a sure success, and only the charms you switch on.
  • P4 is a level-4 item with perfect stats; L4 is a level-4 item below perfect. Each one shows the exact stats it needs.
  • ⚠ test this marks a refine the game might land one point off. Do those first.
  • Key step marks a refine that makes a perfect item from one below perfect. That is where the savings come from, so check the game's preview matches before you refine.

If a result comes out different

In the guide, set the game's numbers on the result with − and +, or tap the pencil on a step in the plan. PORE re-plans the rest from what you have, tells you what changed, and can finish a little lower instead of making items again. A result higher than planned never hurts: keep your plan. The Safe switch plans with a little margin, so no refine can come up short. The game keeps small values its screen doesn't show, so two items that are both below perfect can land one point lower than expected; Safe counts on that.

Items you already have

Add refined copies you own and PORE uses them for free wherever they fit. Counts you enter in Inventory show whether you can do a plan right now.

Good to know

Everything is saved on this device only. Item details come from the Pixel Odyssey wiki; the pictures are PORE's own. PORE is unofficial and not made by the game's developers.
